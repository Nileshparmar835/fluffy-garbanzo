# Script scorecard · 20 points

Give each row 0, 1 or 2. Zero means missing or contradicted. One means present, but a first-time viewer needs an explanation. Two means the script and shot evidence pass the test below. This score measures readiness for production. Completion and swipe behavior are measured after release.

| Criterion | Award two points when… |
|---|---|
| Hook | The first spoken thought gives the viewer a concrete object, an unusual fact or a choice they want to resolve. |
| Honest promise | The hook describes a real event or rule in the story, and the ending fulfills it. |
| Rules | A first-time viewer can repeat the constraint after a short spoken line and one visible demonstration. |
| Fair clues | All clues needed to distinguish the correct answer arrive before voting closes. |
| Escalation | Each action eliminates a possibility, changes the stakes or creates a new prediction. |
| Participation | Alternatives are legible, equally plausible in presentation and followed by a quiet guess beat. |
| Spoken Hindi | The line sounds like someone addressing one person. It fits a natural breath and the chosen narrator handles the words clearly. |
| Word-to-action staging | Each named object, person or decisive verb has a feasible entrance, gesture, movement or reveal anchored to a specific word. |
| Visual proof | The reveal demonstrates why the answer follows. A viewer watching silently can follow the decisive action. |
| Closing callback | The last line adds a consequence or reinterpretation of the first image and leaves room for it to land. |

18–20: record and test a rough cut. 14–17: rewrite the weakest beat and rescore. 0–13: revisit the premise and proof. A zero on honest promise or fair clues requires a story correction even when other rows score highly.

## Worked example

Brief: nine identical-looking sweets; one is heavier, two balance weighings allowed. Candidate 1 asks “दो बार तौलकर भारी लड्डू ढूँढ लोगे?” Candidate 2 starts “नौ में से आठ एक जैसे वज़न के हैं।” Candidate 3 opens “ये नौ लड्डू देखो। एक में डबल खोया है। सिर्फ़ दो बार तौलकर बता सकते हो, कौन सा?” Candidate 3 names something the camera can show, gives it a hidden difference and ends with a challenge. Retain the approved recording for the example episode.

The first weighing compares two groups of three. A tie identifies the outside group; a tilt identifies the heavier group. From the surviving group, compare two singles. A tie identifies the unweighed sweet; a tilt identifies the heavier single. For this story branch, show both ties and let the audience choose among the last three numbers. End on the unweighed sweet being found.

An example pre-release assessment gives 2 each for the hook, honest promise, rules, fair clues, escalation, word-to-action staging, visual proof and closing callback. Give 1 for participation because the final guess window is brief, and 1 for spoken Hindi pending a listener’s pronunciation check. Total: 18/20. If viewers miss the choice, test a slightly longer hold before the reveal and compare the retention curves at that moment.

For each new draft, record the criterion scores and one specific rewrite. For each finished episode, compare the opening and guess/reveal moments against actual audience retention if available. Never invent measured results.
